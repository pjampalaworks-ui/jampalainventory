Privacy Policy — JampalaInventory

Effective Date: November 2026 • Private / Unlisted Mobile Distribution (iOS & Android)

1. Stored on This Phone

JampalaInventory keeps your shop stock lists, bills, daily sales, and holiday and festival reminder settings in a private app database on your phone. Your sign-in and any key you add are kept in the phone’s secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). To sign in, the app makes a random phone ID on your phone and keeps it there; it reaches the owner only when you send it. When the owner renews your access, the app may download one small renewal file over an encrypted HTTPS connection, named by a scrambled form of that ID; it sends no name, no shop data and not the ID itself. We do not run a cloud database of your shop data. On iPhone and iPad, your phone’s own encrypted device backup may include app data, depending on your backup settings; on Android, your phone backup and phone-to-phone transfer carry only the app’s own encrypted backup file, never the shop database itself. Bill photos are deleted from the app as soon as they are read. If something goes wrong inside the app, it keeps a short note of the error on this phone (the time, the app version and the kind of error, never your shop records); you can share or clear these notes in Settings → Privacy & Security, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it. If the owner sets an Owner PIN, the staff names the owner adds and the app’s activity record (who saved each bill, count, print and change, and when) stay on this phone with the shop data, are never collected, and are erased with Delete Account & Wipe Data. Backup files are encrypted so that only JampalaInventory can open them; if you set a backup passphrase, nobody can open them without it, including us. Automatic backups are kept in the cloud drive that comes with your phone, under that provider’s terms; nothing goes to the developer.

2. Permissions

  • Camera (Optional): Requested only when you choose to photograph a printed invoice or counter sale slip.
  • System Photo & File Pickers: Screenshots and Excel (.xlsx / .csv) files are opened through built-in one-time system pickers without full photo-gallery read access.
  • Zero Location / GPS Tracking: Regional currency formatting and holiday and festival reminders work from the region you choose for each shop and the system locale—never GPS.
  • Zero Ad Tracking: JampalaInventory contains zero advertising SDKs, zero analytics trackers, and sets NSPrivacyTracking = false.

3. Reading Bills

By default, bills are read on your phone only. If you add your own Google Gemini API key and turn on the agreement in Settings → Bill Reading, each bill photo you scan is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to Google (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) to read the items. Bill photos can show supplier or customer names, phone numbers, and prices. The app asks Google not to store the request, but how long Google keeps data (for example for abuse checks), and whether data sent with a free key may be used to improve Google’s services, is decided by Google’s own Gemini API terms for your key. You can remove the key and the agreement at any time.

4. Deleting Your Account & Data

You can permanently erase your sign-in, keys, scheduled reminders, any leftover temporary photo or export copies, the error notes, and every shop’s stock and sales on this phone, at any time, inside the app at: Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account & Wipe Data.

5. Shops in the United Kingdom

If your shop is in the UK, UK data protection law (the UK GDPR) applies. In plain words:

  • Your data stays on your phone. Your shop’s stock, bills and sales are kept on the phone and are not sent to the developer.
  • Google bill reading only with your agreement. A bill photo is sent to Google only if you add your own key and agree in Settings, as described in section 3.
  • Your rights. You have the right to know what personal data is held about you, to get a copy, and to have it corrected or erased. Because the data is on your phone, you can see, change, and erase all of it yourself in the app, including with Delete Account & Wipe Data.
  • Contacting the developer. Email supportemail.ji@gmail.com.
  • Complaints. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
